## Prism Cam for Android 1.0.0 — first release

A minimal, premium phone / tablet camera, portrait and landscape. Opens in the light Classic look; Prism glass is one tap away.

**Photo / video**
- Full-resolution photos with zero shutter lag · 4:3 / 16:9 / 1:1 · 3 / 10 s timer · volume keys shoot
- 1080p 30 fps video · pause / resume · photo while recording · **flip the camera mid-recording (one clip)** · hold the shutter to record
- Tap to focus + drag for brightness · pinch zoom · double-tap to flip · swipe to change mode
- Grid + level · clean screen (only the shutter, where it was; fades when idle) · left-handed layout

**✦ Effects (live on screen, burned into photos and videos)**
- 11 looks · text in 4 styles and 8 colours · 24 stickers · 7 frames — drag / pinch / turn them on the picture

**On-device AI (pictures never leave the device)**
- Background blur / replace · portrait mode · 6 face stickers · peace sign ✌️ to shoot

**Send to Prism Cam on the PC** — scan the QR from the PC app and Discord on the PC sees a real camera

**Install:** download `Prism-Cam-1.0.0.apk` on the phone and open it (Android asks once to allow installs from that source) · Android 8+ · later versions update from inside the app (… → About → Check for updates)
