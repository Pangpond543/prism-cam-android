# Prism Cam for Android

A minimal, premium camera for Android phones and tablets — part of the Prism suite by 543.
Photo and video, live effects, text / stickers / frames, on-device AI (background blur / replace, portrait,
face stickers, peace-sign shutter), and it can be the camera of **Prism Cam on Windows**
([Pangpond543/prism-cam](https://github.com/Pangpond543/prism-cam)).

**Download:** the `.apk` from [Releases](https://github.com/Pangpond543/prism-cam-android/releases/latest) · Android 8 or newer.
Updates are offered inside the app (… → About → Check for updates).

ภาษาไทย: กล้องมือถือ / แท็บเล็ตแบบเรียบหรูในชุด Prism — ดาวน์โหลดไฟล์ `.apk` จากหน้า Releases แล้วเปิดบนเครื่อง

Built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, CameraX, OpenGL ES and MediaPipe (models bundled: selfie segmenter,
face landmarker, gesture recognizer — Apache 2.0). Font: IBM Plex Sans Thai (SIL OFL, `licenses/`).
Everything runs on the device; the app only goes online to check GitHub for updates and, when you ask for it,
to connect to Prism Cam on your own PC over your Wi-Fi.
